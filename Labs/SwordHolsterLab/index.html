Control guide
D/S = drawing (unsheathing) / sheathing. One authored second = 60 ticks. Reaching is limited by both linear and angular speed. Sheathing brings the blade tip within 2 cm of the entrance and within its rotation tolerance, then aligns during insertion. Drawing retains its 2 cm / 5° hand arrival gate. A fully stretched arm can stall before the target. Draw starts with the sword seated; Sheathe starts with it held.

This lab models pose and reach. The holster follows the pelvis with optional lab-only rotation stabilization; it does not simulate Jolt collisions, motor lag or NN feedback. Right-arm collision is off during D/S in Unreal.
