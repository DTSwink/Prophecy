Control guide
D/S = drawing (unsheathing) / sheathing. One authored second = 60 ticks. Reaching is limited by both linear and angular speed. Sliding begins inside the Unreal 2 cm / 5° arrival gate. A fully stretched arm can stall before the target. Draw starts with the sword seated; Sheathe starts with it held.

This lab models pose and reach. The holster follows its pelvis rest transform; it does not simulate Jolt collisions, motor lag or NN feedback. Right-arm collision is off during D/S in Unreal.
