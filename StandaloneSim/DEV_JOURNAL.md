Their shared maximum local turn speed is persistent and hot-applied without restarting the run, defaulting to 360 degrees per second. The current lightweight combat pitch uses the accepted rig's 1.495376-meter reference head height transformed by the existing grounded-state pose.
- All agents start equipped with sheathed swords. At Attack activation each draws before running. Sheathe and unsheathe use the same central timed-action path at exactly 1.7 times their former speed: configured durations are `1.6 / 1.7` and `1.4 / 1.7` seconds, then quantized to the 30 Hz fixed step.

Against any living target on the floor (`Agonising`, `PassedOut`, or `Crawling`), a club holder with a usable equipped sword first completes the timed club drop, then draws and uses the real sword; if no usable sword exists, only an authored left/right kick is eligible. A defender already attacking likewise cannot parry or dodge. All attacks launched on the same fixed step choose their defense outcome from the same immutable start-tick batch. Otherwise 20 percent of incoming attacks choose `hit`; the remaining 80 percent uses the configurable parry-versus-dodge split, defaulting to total probabilities of 20 percent hit, 40 percent parry, and 40 percent dodge.

Parry cancels the attack's gameplay result, but its attack animation remains active simultaneously with the parry for the original duration; when that interval ends, parry always applies the configurable 1.5-second parried cooldown. Dodge does not block this follow-up roll.

Each such cooldown also receives one deterministic 50-percent strafe roll, one fixed left/right side, and a seeded travel target from 0.5 to 1.0 meters. A successful roll adds a target-orbit tangent only until actual root displacement along that tangent reaches its travel target. Retreat, strafe, and existing tactical steering combine into one normalized stick through the normal mover, so retreat and strafe can occur simultaneously without collision or a second locomotion path. Grounded finishing explicitly bypasses cooldown retreat, strafe, sectors, and ally separation: the attacker approaches directly when out of range and remains still when in range.

Endpoint hits due on the same tick are collected before effects, so equal-duration simultaneous hits both land; an earlier hit removes a later attack and its pending result.

When stun ends, an injured-leg agent resumes Slow and an otherwise mobile agent resumes Normal.

Grounded hit location remains 80-percent torso and 20-percent head, and the standing-target sword-versus-melee option does not weaken these finisher restrictions.

Against any living defender on the floor (`Agonising`, `PassedOut`, or `Crawling`) it selects only torso or head, using a deterministic 80 percent torso / 20 percent head split. Dead defenders are not targeted. This adds no per-agent timer or allocation.
