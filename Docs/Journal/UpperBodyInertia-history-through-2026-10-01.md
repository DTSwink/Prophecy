**Alpha** (default1, clamped0–1) controls the whole node's influence independently of Momentum Scale. The effective inertia weight is `Alpha * (1 - smoothstep(return blend))`; it affects core rotations, the final solved arm correction and the preserved upper handoff. Alpha-only setter updates preserve existing spring motion and elapsed hold/blend time. Alpha0 cancels active inertia, disables future returns and performs no inertia pose work.
