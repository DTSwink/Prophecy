# Soft angular limits: bounded extension decision

9 September 2026. Read-only feasibility review of UE 5.7.4 and pinned Jolt 5.6.0 (`e77f175595e64cb44218cc9d9d56fc365ad0e36a`). No solver code, dependency, asset, build or runtime changes were made for this note. The runtime facts below were supplied by the parent task's current Chaos benchmark; the older [effective-rig audit](<C:/Users/singerie/Documents/Unreal Projects/Prophecy/Docs/JoltEffectiveRigAudit.md>) provides the source/history context.

**User decision, 9 September 2026:** use stock Jolt **hard angular limits with the authored PHAT angles**, with no retuning. Preserve Locked/Free/Limited selections. A custom soft constraint is deferred unless needed later. The user explicitly accepted the change in compliance; this does not authorize freeing limited axes or changing their angle values. The feasibility review below is retained for that possible later extension, not an open implementation gate.

## Confirmed scope

The current native FullSim benchmark has 22 bodies and 21 valid anatomical joints. Every swing/twist axis is Limited at 179 degrees and both soft flags are true. Observed stiffness/damping conversion scales are 100000/1000; these are **multipliers**, not the final per-joint coefficients. HalfSim NativeWorld and Passive configure all anatomical angular axes Free. The manual follower's effective SCS/runtime articulation remains a separate ongoing audit; this note does not give it FullSim's 179-degree configuration. The authored rigs also contain Locked twists and narrower limits, so the eventual retained importer must report coverage by actual motion/profile combination.

A 179-degree limit is not automatically Free in Jolt. Its internal free threshold is greater than 179.5 degrees on both sides. Nor does an ordinary motion sample staying inside the limits prove that removing them preserves the functionality. ## Fidelity questions a generic spring patch does not settle

**Converted coefficients and force mode.** UE multiplies each authored swing/twist stiffness and damping by the observed scales, and converts limit degrees to radians. The ordinary physics-interface setters do not set `AngularSoftForceMode`; that assignment is commented out. Chaos joint defaults select Acceleration. Capture the actual joint field, solver overrides and global disable-soft-limits state rather than inferring them solely from a CVar. [PhysInterface_Chaos.cpp:337](<C:/Program Files/Epic Games/UE_5.7/Engine/Source/Runtime/Engine/Private/PhysicsEngine/Experimental/PhysInterface_Chaos.cpp:337>), [:359](<C:/Program Files/Epic Games/UE_5.7/Engine/Source/Runtime/Engine/Private/PhysicsEngine/Experimental/PhysInterface_Chaos.cpp:359>), [PBDJointConstraints.cpp:350](<C:/Program Files/Epic Games/UE_5.7/Engine/Source/Runtime/Experimental/Chaos/Private/Chaos/PBDJointConstraints.cpp:350>), [PBDJointConstraintUtilities.cpp:405](<C:/Program Files/Epic Games/UE_5.7/Engine/Source/Runtime/Experimental/Chaos/Private/Chaos/PBDJointConstraintUtilities.cpp:405>)

For confirmed Acceleration mode, Jolt's `MassNormalizedStiffnessAndDamping` is the corresponding physical parameter convention: effective inertia multiplies the coefficients. Do not apply a cm-to-m torque multiplier to mass-normalized angular coefficients. For Force mode, angular stiffness/damping instead require the squared length conversion from kg·cm² to kg·m², in addition to UE's profile scales. Both mappings still need the response comparison below. A damping-only soft setting requires care: `CalculateConstraintPropertiesWithSettingsForLimit` treats zero stiffness as hard even when damping is positive. The more general spring entry points support positive damping with zero stiffness. **Swing geometry.** The UE cached/linear solver uses two swing rows when both swing axes are Limited. Its scalar and all-Limited/all-soft SIMD paths use component angles `4*atan2(qSwing.y or z, 1+qSwing.w)`. Jolt Cone clamps an ellipse in quaternion y/z coordinates; Jolt Pyramid uses `2*atan2(qSwing.y or z, qSwing.w)`. These yield different activation boundaries for combined swing, particularly near the observed wide limits. Merely selecting Jolt Pyramid or copying the angle numbers is insufficient. The UE nonlinear solver has a separate elliptical-cone path; confirm which path is active. **Solver response.** Chaos's soft correction is position based and includes step-scaled stiffness/damping, accumulated lambda, solver stiffness, and rotation-delta-derived damping. The cached path can disable the position-based damping term through its solver configuration. Jolt's reusable part applies velocity impulses and warm starts them. Activation tolerance, limit-side changes, return velocity, unilateral impulse bounds, 180-degree degeneracy, iteration ordering, conditioning and projection therefore require measurements. The inspected Chaos scalar soft solve does not have Jolt Hinge's unilateral accumulated-impulse clamp; selecting that clamp would be an additional behavior choice. ## Acceptance before a faithful-port claim

1. Freeze the actual converted rig/profile, local frames, inertia, soft force mode and solver configuration, including manual follower observations. Keep its physics cadence and NN cadence unchanged.
2. Compare one joint at rest inside its bounds, just inside/outside each bound, and returning through it. Record angle, angular velocity, row impulse, maximum excursion and decay over time. Include both dynamic/dynamic and fixed/dynamic bodies and unequal inertias.
3. Exercise simultaneous swing/twist, diagonal swing and both sides near 179/180 degrees, including equivalent quaternion signs. Verify limit activation against the selected Chaos geometry; guard singularities without silently replacing them by Free behavior.
4. Check zero damping, positive damping, any actually used zero-stiffness case, mode/profile changes, sleep/wake and removal/recreation. No stale warm-start impulse may survive a changed side/frame/profile. Locked axes retain their hard constraint; Free axes receive no angular stop impulse.
5. Compare the 21-joint rig under retained targets and impacts with contacts, production iterations and conditioning enabled. Measure translation attachment error and pose/velocity response alongside CPU cost. Establish numerical/visual acceptance tolerances from Chaos captures before deciding that remaining differences are acceptable.

**Deferred extension:** the original recommendation was a bounded project-owned soft angular prototype and its Chaos comparison. The user instead selected hard limits above. Retain the listed response tests if soft constraints become necessary; neither upstream modification nor exact behavioral equivalence has been established as necessary.
