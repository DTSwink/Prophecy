# Locomotion-authored feet during full attacks

**Set Attack Foot Locomotion** configures Enabled, Mode (Walk / Run / Current Blend), Distance Limit Cm (default 40), Height Limit Cm (default 15), and **Freeze Blend** (default 0). Disabled by default; configure before triggering the attack. Current Blend continues the ordinary gait blend instead of resetting it at full attack entry. Walk and Run select their respective lower checkpoint.

Additional pins: **Alpha Rotation** (default1, clamped0–1), **Left Blend Duration Seconds** and **Right Blend Duration Seconds** (both default0). Alpha Rotation1 uses locomotion foot orientation;0 uses attack orientation while the foot position remains locomotion-authored. Toe articulation follows the same rotational source, attached to the chosen ankle. These settings latch at attack entry, alongside mode/limits; Freeze Blend remains live adjustable.

**Knee Pole Blend Duration Seconds** (default0, disabled) optionally blends the knee bend direction in foot-local space. A positive duration starts immediately at drag entry for each locomotion-owned foot, independent of the distance/height handoff. Each sample takes the locomotion pole relative to the locomotion foot and the attack pole relative to the attack foot, blends those directions along a spherical arc, then transforms the result by the final Alpha Rotation-controlled foot orientation. The knee rotates around the hip-to-ankle axis, preserving hip, ankle, both segment lengths and foot/toe transforms. Opposite local poles use the source leg axis to choose a stable arc; degenerate straight-leg/parallel-pole cases retain the current solution.

Pole timing uses60 unpaused ticks per second, pauses during half attacks, and never starts for direct-attack feet. Once the timer finishes, the dragging foot uses the current attack-local pole without further clock work. If a positive position handoff finishes sooner, it also brings the pole to attack using `attackPoleAlpha = 1 - remainingLocomotionWeight * (1 - timedPoleAlpha)`. Existing instantaneous position handoffs retain their instantaneous semantics. Pole duration0 leaves the previous knee behavior intact and allocates no pole state/clock or additional pole solve. No extra inference is introduced; all pole state retires with the foot handoff/end/reset/disable.

When a pending foot enters both limits, its positive-duration handoff starts at full locomotion position and smoothstep blends toward the current attack pose. Its rotational locomotion contribution is `AlphaRotation * remainingLocomotionWeight`. Each foot starts/finishes on its own clock, using60 unpaused game ticks per second; moving back outside cannot cancel or restart that handoff. Feet already accepted at attack entry bypass blending entirely. Zero duration preserves the existing final locomotion feedback sample and immediate ownership release. The connected knee solve preserves segment lengths through positive-duration blends.

Pending/blending feet keep their existing lower locomotion request until the last fade finishes; there is no additional model evaluation beyond retaining the required source during that interval. Configured foot inertia begins at that foot's completed handoff. Get Attack Foot Locomotion remains true during its fade; debug draws orange **LOCO -> ATTACK** while blending. Half mode pauses those clocks and suspends drag; full rejoin resumes remaining time without reacquiring completed feet. Disabling, switching to a kick, ending/resetting the attack or world cleanup cancels the handoff state. Default rotation1/durations0 allocates no extension state or clock and retains the original copy/hip-connect path; disabled/completed feet perform no blend work beyond existing cheap guards.

**Freeze Blend** is clamped to 0–1 and can change during active loco drag. Zero preserves the ordinary root prediction. One collapses the future positional trajectory directly beneath the current NN pelvis (pelvis XY, root floor Z); intermediate values interpolate each position toward that point. Yaw and sample times stay unchanged. The blended trajectory feeds the lower NN and advances its mover consistently. The continuous root-window getter applies the same blend to all nine positions, including index 0, using the currently presented NN pelvis; it retains the original future offsets to avoid blending twice. This uses the authored NN pelvis, not the physical body. At zero/inactive, no additional pelvis sampling or window capture runs; no extra inference is introduced.

**Kicks (`kickl`, `kickr`) are excluded from loco drag.** They take attack ownership of both feet at entry, with configured foot inertia starting immediately. Switching an ongoing attack to a kick releases any remaining drag feet and starts their configured inertia. The drag configuration remains available for later non-kick attacks. **Get Continuous Locomotion Root Window** is available while a full non-kick attack still has a drag foot; completed full attacks return false again.

At full entry, each foot outside either limit starts locomotion-owned. Distance applies only behind the pelvis along the current horizontal pelvis-to-target axis: `dot(foot - pelvis, axis) < -DistanceLimitCm`. Forward and lateral distance are unrestricted. Height is ankle height above the agent root floor and still applies in every direction. Once inside both limits (including equality), that foot permanently starts its handoff to attack, immediate by default or using the configured duration. Retargeting updates the axis but cannot reacquire a released foot. Full-to-half suspends this feature; returning to full preserves its completed feet. A pure half attack uses ordinary locomotion.

The attack retains its pelvis and upper body. Pending legs use the selected locomotion output, connected to the attack hips with the existing length-preserving knee solve. Full-attack root movement and physical profiles retain their existing behavior. This feature does not turn a full attack into a moving half attack.

Locomotion-owned/blending legs feed their accepted foot position/rotation, thigh rotation and toe angle back into the attack's current lower state, expressed in the fixed attack carrier, and into the ordinary lower locomotion recurrence. The usual recurrent shift preserves their previous sample. Zero-duration release displays/feeds the final locomotion sample; positive-duration completion reaches the attack pose before ownership retires. Completed feet receive no further locomotion feedback. This is authored pose feedback, not physical-body feedback; independent half-attack ghosts remain unchanged. The feedback itself requires no additional network inference, pose history or allocation, and runs only while a foot is pending/blending.

**Set Attack Start Foot Inertia** starts independently for each foot when that foot becomes attack-owned. Feet already inside the limits, or attacks without this feature, start inertia immediately at full entry. Delayed feet retain their entire configured window; releasing one does not restart the other's clock. Disabling authoring releases any pending feet and begins their inertia immediately. Configuring again during the attack does not reacquire released feet.

**Get Attack Foot Locomotion** returns Left Locomotion / Right Locomotion. **Draw Attack Foot Locomotion** draws the yellow rear distance limit, an unrestricted-forward green arrow, the white height limit, and cyan LOCO / red ATTACK markers. Call it explicitly while debugging; it installs no tick or timer.

Only pending feet keep the lower locomotion policy request alive. The last release removes that request for subsequent inference steps; upper locomotion remains skipped during full attacks. Other agents may still require the shared fixed-size batch. Disabled/completed authoring does no extra inference, threshold math, pose capture, or leg solve beyond cheap guards. Configuration/reset/EndPlay/world cleanup follow the existing agent lifecycle.
