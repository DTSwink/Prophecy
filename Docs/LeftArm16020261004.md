# Left-arm hesitation after slashLU — October 4

The current setup has a real slowdown, not repeated/frozen left-arm poses. Around
ticks 162–168 the locomotion NN decelerates its hand trajectory toward a swing
direction change, then accelerates through 172–178. The raw checkpoint prediction
already contains this motion. This establishes where the motion originates; it
does not establish a defect in the weights independent of their input/history.

## Captures and timing

Four complete owned 260-tick replays used the current TestNN/PoseAgent setup.
`bool debug 3` was already true; the baseline did not override it. The agent is
Kinematic during the relevant interval. slashLU starts at 90, becomes half, and
ends at 131; the next attack starts at 210.

The current FK return has coefficient 2, easing .5, inertia .5 and trim .35.
Its current endpoint reaches full NN at tick 150; the preceding interpolation
endpoint remains until 151. From 152 onward the return is absent. Attack motion
inertia and entry hand/core inertia are also absent at the reported hesitation.
Forearm stretch recovery is finished by then. The root moves 8.333 cm/tick from
150 onward; there is no root halt. Upper inference runs at every normal 30 Hz
boundary throughout 146–192.

Baseline displayed left-hand displacement relative to the pelvis is 3.576 cm at
160, 2.007 at 164, 1.021 at 168 and 7.783 at 178 (per 60 Hz game tick). At 168 the
upperarm still rotates 2.713 degrees relative to its clavicle and the lowerarm
1.843 degrees relative to the upperarm. Thus this is not a frozen bone frame.

## Controlled comparisons

- **Trace instrumentation:** full captured state equals the untraced baseline
  exactly. The native trace does not change this rollout.
- **FK return disabled at 129:** the pose prefix through 129 is exact, no FK
  return appears afterward, and all captured lower/root data remain exact. The
  early exit trajectory changes, but the later 166–168 hesitation remains.
- **Free wrist from 151:** all lower/root data remain exact and the arm's local
  rotation traces remain identical over the symptom. Freeing the NN wrist removes
  reference-length attachment, but retains the slowdown. The attachment affects
  hand position slightly; it is not the source of the pause-like interval.
- **Checkpoint evaluated in Python:** actual captured inputs reproduce Unreal's
  upper output deltas within 4.21e-7. With wrist freedom, raw hand displacement
  relative to the future pelvis matches the displayed future endpoint within
  4.24e-5 cm in radius (154–200), with matching step lengths after the toggle
  transition. The raw hand takes 7.342, 3.390, 1.866 and 16.898 cm steps at policy
  ticks 160, 164, 168 and 176 respectively (each step spans two game ticks).

The raw pelvis-local hand trajectory changes direction by 114 degrees between
the steps ending at 166 and 168. A similar swing reversal recurs around 204–206.
The return can shape the earlier post-attack settling and subsequent NN history,
but removing it does not remove this later slowdown. No lingering inertia timer,
missed inference, physical solver hold or display-only freeze was found.
