## Physical Simulation And Magnetization

- Physical mode is real Chaos simulation: rigid bodies travel under forces/torques and constraints and can collide during travel. It does not teleport limb colliders to authored targets.
- Current manual follower uses absolute-world magnetization in the Chaos pre-integrate callback. Each included simulated body receives position and orientation correction toward the finalized data-only target.
- Per-body settings are stored in `Body Magnetization Settings`:
  - `bSimulateBody` includes/excludes the body when Physical mode is initialized.
  - `bMagnetizationEnabled` enables the native follower for that body.
  - `LinearStrengthScale` and `AngularStrengthScale`: `1.0` exact accepted correction, `0.5` half correction, `0.0` disables that correction axis.
  - Missing entries deliberately default to enabled/`1.0`/`1.0`.
- Runtime Blueprint controls: `Set All Body Magnetization`, `Set Body Magnetization`, `Set Body Magnetization Below`, `Set Body Included In Physical Simulation`, settings query, one-body configured/custom world magnetization, and automatic publication/application gates.
- `My Set Physics Asset` replaces the active PHAT at runtime and rebuilds its articulation while preserving matching bodies by bone name: world transform, linear/angular velocity, simulation, gravity, physics blend, and awake/sleep state. It also reapplies collision, solver iterations, MACD, drive topology, and live manual-follower bindings without modifying magnetization settings. The accepted equivalent-PHAT round trip preserved all 22 matching bodies with `0 cm` position/linear/angular-velocity error, `0` simulation-state changes, unchanged 9/9 magnetization entries, and only `0.0000025 deg` quaternion-rounding error; the follower remained active across later physics ticks.
- Current per-body Chaos solver defaults are 4 position, 1 velocity, and 0 projection iterations. The retained constraints provide articulation and impact propagation; native lower-body joint motors are not used by the absolute-world follower.
- MACD is applied per rigid body. Project `p.Chaos.Solver.UseMACD` permits it. Leave MACD on by default; the accepted 100-agent test did not show a saving from disabling it.
- Enhanced determinism remains on, async physics off, substepping off. No global Chaos optimization CVar from the prior audit is saved.
- Skeletal ground contacts remain disabled in the focused controller experiment until explicitly restored; the capsule remains the root support. Do not silently re-enable contacts.
- `prophecy.Physical.AuditManualFollower 1` enables the off-by-default numerical audit comparing each body at the start of PrePhysics with the exact target saved after the preceding PrePhysics tick. Disabled means no sampling/logging cost.
- Physical recurrent feedback is sampled from realized Chaos transforms at each 30 Hz step. Lower recurrent pelvis/thigh/foot/toe channels and Upper recurrent core/clavicle/upper-arm/hand channels are independently filtered by their named linear/angular tolerances before becoming the next state.

## Accepted Performance And Physics Decisions

- Four foot-roll steps cost about `0.4140 ms` for all 100 agents per NN update in the retained CPU/NullRHI sweep. Fixed output work at zero steps was about `0.1693 ms`; 64 steps cost `3.9268 ms`.
- `Docs/AgentSimulationCostAudit.md` is the retained 100-agent 0/50/100 Physical audit at 30 Hz:
  - Median total CPU/headless frame: `5.021 / 14.552 / 29.894 ms`.
  - Median Chaos solver: `0.238 / 4.983 / 12.663 ms`.
  - All-Physical resampling adds about `0.144 ms`; output/IK remains about `0.44-0.47 ms`; pose publishing about `0.14-0.15 ms`.
- Earlier joint-torque topology was substantially cheaper than independent per-body world constraints and justified retaining articulation constraints. Current manual follower uses simple absolute-world force/torque magnetization while constraints retain articulation.
- Physical agents retain one Chaos `PreIntegrate` callback per agent. A manager-owned callback with one target publication per frame was rejected in the controlled 100-Physical-agent CPU/NullRHI test: median frame time was `127.157 ms` batched versus `119.814 ms` per-agent, and median `EndPhysics` wait was `98.076 ms` versus `93.182 ms`. The single callback serialized all body-target writes on the physics critical path instead of letting Chaos distribute the per-agent callbacks.
- `Docs/ChaosPhysicsOptimizationReport_UE5_7.md` records the Chaos audit. Task-based result push plus Partial-Jacobi showed about 5% solver CPU improvement but is not saved because contact behavior still needs validation. Async Chaos, PhysicsOnly collision, and disabling enhanced determinism were not keeper gains.
- Production remains on Chaos. `Docs/PhysicsBackendResearch_2026-08-11.md` records why Box3D is not a drop-in replacement despite promising raw ragdoll capacity: the current plugin lacks SkeletalMesh/PhysicsAsset/Physical Animation and Chaos interaction integration.
- The current project can be capped by Unreal's laptop-on-battery 60 FPS behavior; `r.DontLimitOnBattery=1` removes that editor cap for controlled profiling. Do not confuse it with simulation cost.
