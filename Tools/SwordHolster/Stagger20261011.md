Seven alternating paired repeats after warmup, 100 independent agents. Includes initial pose math on admission ticks. Excludes NN reads/inference, skeleton discovery, cache publication, equipment, physics and rendering.

Staggering spreads work across ticks; it is not a reduction in the amount of animation work. Mean includes the slightly longer crowd window caused by delayed starts, so total CPU time is also retained in the JSON.
